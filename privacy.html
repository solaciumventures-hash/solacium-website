Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Solacium Ventures Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under applicable data protection law.

Information we collect through this website

We do not collect any personal information through this website unless you choose to contact us directly. Any information you provide is used solely to respond to your inquiries and will not be shared with third parties.

We do not use cookies or tracking technologies on this site.

Email signup

If you submit your email address, we collect and store that information for the purpose of sending you updates on upcoming products. You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us.

App data collection

When you use our apps, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: name, email, authentication provider
  • App preferences: categories, notifications, themes
  • Usage data: favorite affirmations, settings
  • Device information: crash reporting, notifications
  • Subscription and purchase data: processed through RevenueCat to manage your subscription and unlock premium features

Your data is stored securely using Firebase services with encryption in transit and at rest.

Affiliate attribution: If you install one of our apps through a creator or affiliate link, we use a third-party service, GoMarketMe, to attribute that referral. A device identifier and your in-app purchase records are shared with GoMarketMe for this purpose — to credit the referring affiliate and calculate commission. This is used for attribution measurement only; it is not used for advertising, and we do not access Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA). GoMarketMe's privacy policy is available at gomarketme.co.

Account deletion

If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please use our contact form with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account. Upon verification, all your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Your data rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by using the contact form.

Legal basis and data retention

We process your personal data on the legal basis of consent in accordance with the UK GDPR. We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the stated purpose or until you withdraw consent.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. We share limited data only with the service providers needed to run our apps — for example, Firebase (secure storage and authentication), RevenueCat (subscription and purchase management), and GoMarketMe (affiliate-referral attribution, as described above) — and only for those purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
