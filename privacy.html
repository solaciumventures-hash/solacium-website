Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

Solacium ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to being transparent about how we handle your information.

What we collect

We keep data collection to an absolute minimum. The only information we collect through this website is what you voluntarily provide in our contact form: your name, email address, and the message you send us.

What we don't collect

  • No cookies or tracking
  • No analytics
  • No browsing behavior logging
  • No device information or IP addresses
  • No third-party tracking pixels

How we use your information

Information you submit through our contact form is used solely to respond to your message. We will never sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party services

We use Formspree to process contact form submissions and GitHub Pages to host this website. Both may process standard server logs as part of their operation.

Your rights

You have the right to access any personal data we hold about you, request its deletion, or withdraw consent for communications at any time.

Data retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and keep a reasonable record of our correspondence.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
